Interests

Video Games

Geometry Dash

Geometry Dash is a rhythm-based platformer game that I have been playing for a couple years now. It only has one button, and yet is somehow so challenging so often. The community is so creative, and it's a little hard to put into words, so I'll recommend this video to get a basic rundown on the game if you want to learn. Or care. I don't know. I just like it.

Roblox - Forsaken

Roblox has been kind of on a decline as of the recent years. While I played it a lot when I was younger and still do play it often in adulthood, I sadly don't find it as engaging anymore. However, I have been playing a game on Roblox called Forsaken, which is an asymetrical "survivors vs. killer" game that I play on essentially the daily, even if not seriously. It's a good game... with a HORRIBLE COMMUNITY AND HORRIBLE PEOPLE RUNNING IT. I swear to god man, this game gets in drama literally every goddamn day, it baffles me how a single game can have THIS much drama and THIS many weirdos. Still, the game itself? I highly recommend. I will say that the game is NOT beginner friendly, though. Ask my friend Elvis.

Death Palette

Here's a niche pull. I've always been into puzzle games—I've been an Angry Birds fan since I was unconscious—and I've never slowed down. This game and another called "I Expect You To Die" are my favorites to pull out whenever people talk about obscure indie games. Basically, Death Palette is a point-and-click survival horror puzzle game where you survive seven days trapped with a cursed painintg of a girl. It released back in, like, 2021, I played it to completion over a week, and I loved it. It's been a couple years since I beat it, but I highly recommend it if you enjoy puzzles. Seriously, it's free on the Play Store, just give it a shot.

Music

Hip-Hop

I like hip-hop. It's cool. It makes me go "haha bom ba-bom" and hit a jiggy and bust a move. Seriously though, I've been really into hip-hop and rap for quite a while now. My favorite artists are Kendrick Lamar and Tyler, The Creator, but my cousin Carter's always trying to get me into other, more niche artists. Like who the hell is Che? If I had to recommend an album to start and help you get into hip-hop... it would be either Section.80, To Pimp A Butterfly or IGOR, by—once again—Kendrick, Kendrick, and Tyler. IGOR is more pop-focused though, so maybe just Section.80. Love that album. Actually, try CALL ME IF YOU GET LOST by Tyler. Another good album.

Jazz

I don't really know how I got into jazz, to be honest. I think I just listened to "Reeds and Seeds" by Ruscel Torres, a jazz reimagining of the Plants vs. Zombies soundtrack, and just decided "yup, I like this genre now." I naturally love both saxophone and piano though, so I guess it's not a big surprise. I don't have many album recommendations except that Reeds and Seeds one I just mentioned, so... also check out LeGrand, I guess?
